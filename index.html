Our Story

A real knitting mill.

We have been knitting for other brands since 2010 — sweaters for labels you would recognize. The machines are ours. The hands are ours. The wool comes straight from the Australian market.

Last year we stopped making clothes for other people's labels and started making them for ours. Same line, same hands — minus the markup.
